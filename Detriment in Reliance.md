# Detriment in Reliance
A promise which could be expected to and does induce action or forbearance is legally enforceable if justice requires (R2K 90), with remedy limited as justice requires. Charitable subscription or marriage settlements are binding without such a promise. This is limited - the promisor must be reasonably aware of the reliance and the promise is only legally enforceable if justice requires. Formality of promise, reasonableness of reliance are among the tools used to determine the latter. A promise binding under this section is a contract - relief can be limited to restitution, damages, or specific relief measured by the promisees reliance rather than the terms.
