## Rule
Inadequacy of consideration does not void a contract - consideration need just exist.

## Holding
The 2k contract was enforceable as the 500k drachma return performance was bargained for and rendered.

## Posture
Plaintiff appealed from the District Court of Bexar County. The trial court awarded $750 and 8% interest.

## Facts
The plaintiff gave 2k USD to support the defendant, who promised to return the above alongside 8% interest at the end of the war or earlier. The defendant gave $25 plus interest post trial. The defendant alleges they only received 500k drachmae, worth $25. The defendant alleged that the written instrument for 2k was extracted out of the defendant's financial duress and want to return to the USA. The defendant alleged that they would only receive the 500k drachmas in return for signing the instrument.

## Issue
Whether 2k + 8% interest was recoverable under the instrument above, or whether it was created in duress and thus is unenforceable.

## Disposition
Reformed and affirmed. The trial court awarded a $750 principle, ammended to the full $2k.

## Reasoning
The defendant knew the condition by which she would receive the 500k drachma, and did it, and thus she got exactly what she bargained for. The 500k drachma were a material benefit and the trial court affirmed this. There was sufficient consideration. Thus, the amount should be paid in full subject to interest.

## Learning Outcome
Limitations of gross inadequacy and duress.
