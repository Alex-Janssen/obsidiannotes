# Contract
A contract is a promise or a set of promises for the breach of which the law gives a remedy, or the performance of which the law in some way recognizes as a duty (R2K 1). 
A promise is a manifestation of intention to act or refrain from acting in a specified way, so made as to justify a promisee in understanding that a commitment has been made (R2K 2 1). A promisor promises to a promisee, and may aid a beneficiary as well.
